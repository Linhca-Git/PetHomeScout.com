# Founder-Led Product Test Rubric

Use one completed record per product and test period before displaying “Founder-tested.” Otherwise label the item `Research-led`.

## Required record

- Product and model; date tested; reviewer; evidence status; last reviewed date.
- Household context: pet type, breed/coat characteristics, pet count, floor type, room layout, and stated job.
- Setup: installation, app/account requirements, accessories, learning curve, and time to reliable use.
- Observed outcome: what happened, how it was observed, timing, and conditions.
- Ownership friction: maintenance, consumables, noise, cleaning, dock work, safety concerns, and recurring cost.
- Limitations: what was not tested, what may vary by household, and conflicts with manufacturer claims.
- Score rationale: household fit, ownership friction, evidence quality, and confidence level.

## Display rules

- Show `Founder-tested` only when every required field is complete.
- Show `Research-led` when advice derives from manufacturer documentation and independent research.
- Never convert one founder household result into a guarantee, universal rating, medical claim, or coverage promise.

# PetHomeScout Affiliate Strategy

Affiliate pages use internal `/go/{merchant}/{product}/` links only. Raw network URLs never appear in article content.

## CTA hierarchy

1. Quick verdict and evidence context.
2. Affiliate buy box or comparison action.
3. Secondary contextual service fallback only when explicitly enabled.

Pending offers render disabled controls. Prices require a source and last-checked date. Live redirects require approved Merchant and Offer records plus the feature flag.

Every commercial page shows the affiliate disclosure below the H1/introduction and before the first commercial action.

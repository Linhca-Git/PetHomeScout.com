# PetHomeScout Internal Linking

Internal links are editor-controlled. Do not inject links by replacing keywords automatically.

## Required Article relationships

- Parent hub
- Related problem guide
- Related comparison
- Related product guide
- Related decision tool
- Related service page
- Methodology page

## Rendered decision blocks

- Continue Your Decision
- Compare Your Options
- DIY or Professional Help
- How We Evaluate
- Related Pet-Home Guides

The service block is rendered after article content and related links, never above the first affiliate CTA or quick verdict. An article without a selected relationship remains free of unrelated service CTAs.

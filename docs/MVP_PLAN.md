# PetHomeScout MVP Plan

## Scope

US-only WordPress review and decision engine for pet-friendly family homes. The MVP validates two monetization paths without live credentials or PII storage:

1. Services & Insurance conversion hub with demo multi-step forms.
2. Smart Tech affiliate comparison and decision-tool path using disabled `/go/` placeholders.

## Approved routes

`/services-insurance/`, `/smart-tech/`, `/cleaning-odor/`, `/family-home/`, `/pet-tech-selector/`, `/methodology/`, `/how-we-test/`, and the representative article/service routes.

## Acceptance criteria

- WordPress-only implementation.
- Flat root URLs and preview `noindex,nofollow`.
- ACF-managed evidence, internal-link, monetization, and hyper-niche fields.
- No direct affiliate URLs, fake testing claims, or stored lead PII.
- Rank Math/Yoast-compatible metadata ownership.
- Responsive, keyboard-accessible, locally validated templates.

## Deferred until staging approval

Live affiliate destinations, lead routing, analytics credentials, consent logging, production indexing, and external provider integrations.

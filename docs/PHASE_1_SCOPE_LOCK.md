# PetHomeScout Phase 1 Scope Lock

Status: active implementation constraint for the U.S. English launch.

## Public Phase 1

- Clean Pet Home: `/cleaning-odor/`
- Pet Hair Cleaning: `/pet-hair-cleaning/`
- Pet Odor & Stain Removal: `/pet-odor-stain-removal/`
- Pet Home Cleaning Selector: `/pet-home-cleaning-selector/`
- Evidence, methodology, disclosure, privacy, terms, contact, and brand trust pages

Primary affiliate categories are robot vacuums, carpet cleaners, enzyme cleaners, and grooming vacuums. Offers stay disabled until a merchant and destination are approved.

The only planned lead fallback is professional pet odor and carpet cleaning. It stays inactive until buyer coverage, consent language, routing, retention, rejection, and payout terms are verified.

## Prepared but not public

- Pet dander and indoor air
- Air purifiers
- General smart-pet technology
- Family Home
- Pet insurance
- Mobile grooming
- Pet sitting, walking, and boarding
- Pet doors, flooring, furniture, and landscaping

These templates and data structures may remain in the codebase. They must not appear in production navigation or sitemaps, receive production internal links, or resolve as public routes before an expansion review.

## Expansion gate

A new hub requires documented evidence of:

1. Sufficient depth and index coverage in the current cluster.
2. Commercial pages earning ranking and click signals.
3. Approved merchants or buyers with backup coverage.
4. Confirmed conversion evidence.
5. A manageable refresh backlog.
6. A direct relationship to the current user's problem journey.

Production indexing remains disabled until launch readiness, legal pages, sitemap contents, analytics ownership, and runtime SEO output are approved.
